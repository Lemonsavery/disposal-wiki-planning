What is atoz currently?
- front end, searchable with fine SEO and alias acceptance.
- list of items, with information for single location, Boulder County
- ability to submit edit suggestions, through suggesitons and contact us email.

What are the pieces that need to be built?
- Frontend, hosted
- massive collection of Pages with default text and images, including links between Pages, hosted
- that massive collection of Page data has a searchstring query tool with great SEO and alias acceptance, hosted server?
- CMS for volunteers, hosted, to:
	> add custom local Pages
	> do Page local edits, verifications
	> CRUD local Depot(s)/Pickup(s)/Event(s), verifications
	> negotiate Region with other volunteers, to determine what "local" means for them (default overrides, CRUD permissions)
	> see/delete local suggestions (maybe also discuss?)
	> see Global Waste Guide styleguide and guidance
	> Global forum (might just be subreddit or child-ranked Discord)
- build wiki-like account permission system, hosted

What questions does a Page answer?
- Is this page about the thing I searched for? (established via name(s) and image(s)). GLOBAL
- Where do I take this thing? What extra steps do I have to take to do so (prices, sign up with city, package it, remove something, find serial number, etc)? LOCAL
- This is interesting, where do I learn more about this stuff? LOCALISH
- This info seems wrong, where do I tell someone to correct it? LOCAL



Components of the Global website/app:
- provide Location
- language chooser
- searchbar
- search results
- Page container
- suggest an edit. (outside of any Page) (should confirm user info: Location, contact info)
- version number in innocuous place (2026.01.31.date?)

Components of a Page:
- title (defautled)
- image (defaulted)
- other text info Global (may have default), has an UpdatedDate only if non-default.
- local Depot(s)/Pickup(s)/Event(s), has an UpdatedDate for the group but not for individual. may have pagination
- before there's depots added, we should have default info for a blank Depot section. It will say that this page hasn't been attended to by local volunteers yet, but here's some general options that are likely to work.
- suggest an edit. (within this Page) (should confirm user info: Location, Page, contact info)

Components of a Depot/Pickup/Event:
- Page specific information (acceptability criteria, prices, etc), has an UpdatedDate
- "information" should have some frontend styling options, since it's so freeform.
- name
- general information (freeform, contact info, dates/times, times of year, entry prices, description, etc), hidden behind a click in some way. has an UpdatedDate
- Location or Region, apart of general information

Components of an UpdatedDate:
- The most recent between edited and verified is shown in small text someplace out of the way. The other two are behind a tooltip alongside, with explaination of what these different things mean.
- "last viewed by Contributor: [Date]" (2026/01/31)
- "last edited by Contributor: [Date]"
- "last verified by Contributor: [Date]"
